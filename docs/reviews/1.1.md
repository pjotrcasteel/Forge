# Forge 1.1 review — Explainable plans

## Capability

Forge can now turn a same-type or cross-type Sync plan into an immutable explanation model.

Updated items include child facts for every Delta property transition. Applications can enrich the structural explanation with their own policy, dependency, prerequisite or execution facts without converting the plan to log strings.

## Boundary

Forge explains structural reconciliation facts. It does not invent domain dependencies or business policy. `PlanExplanationBuilder<TKey>` exists specifically so a consuming planner can attach those facts while keeping ownership clear.

## Why this is a feature

Before 1.1 consumers could inspect buckets and Deltas but had to invent an explanation/audit shape themselves. 1.1 creates a stable structured representation that can drive diagnostics, visualization, approval UI and support tooling.
