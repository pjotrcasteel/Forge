# Forge 1.11 review — dependency-safe plan slicing

## Capability

Consumers can request a subset of a plan while Forge automatically includes every in-plan predecessor required to keep the slice executable.

## SOLID and typing

- Selection policy is consumer-owned through `IPlanSliceSelector<TItem>`.
- Logical identity remains `TKey`; Forge introduces no string keys.
- Direct selection and dependency-closure inclusion are separate immutable result buckets.

## Complexity

The index and closure traversal are O(V + E) for the traversed subgraph. Cycles terminate through the visited key set and do not duplicate items.
